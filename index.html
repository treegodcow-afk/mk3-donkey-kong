Mortal Kombat 3, with Donkey Kong

Load your own dump of Mortal Kombat 3 (USA) and your Donkey Kong Country (USA) (Rev 1) dump. The page draws Donkey Kong from your DKC cartridge into MK3, in Sub-Zero's slot on the select screen (he fights with Sub-Zero's moves). Two players on one keyboard, or invite a friend online. The game files never leave your browser.

You're joining a friend's game as player 2. Their game streams to you here and your controls run player 2. Press Start (Enter) to join in.
